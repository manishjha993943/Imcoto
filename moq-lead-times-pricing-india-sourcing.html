📊 Commercial Basics

MOQ, Lead Times & Pricing: What to Expect Sourcing from India

A grounded overview of how these commercial terms typically work — so you can evaluate a quote with confidence.

MOQ

Minimum Order Quantity

MOQ is set per manufacturer and per product — it depends on their fabric supplier's minimums, production line efficiency, and how the order fits their existing schedule. There's no fixed industry number; always confirm MOQ for your specific product and design rather than assuming a standard figure.

Lead Time

Production & Shipping Timeline

Lead time covers sampling, bulk production, quality check and transit. It varies by product complexity, current factory workload and shipping method (sea freight vs. air). Always get a specific lead time in writing for your order rather than relying on general industry estimates — and build in buffer time for your own launch or retail deadlines.

Pricing Basis

FOB vs. CIF vs. EXW

🏭

EXW (Ex Works)

You arrange and pay for transport from the factory gate onward, including export clearance. Lowest quoted price, most responsibility on you.

🚢

FOB (Free on Board)

The exporter delivers to the port and handles export clearance; you arrange shipping from the port onward. The most common basis for garment exports.

📦

CIF (Cost, Insurance, Freight)

The exporter arranges shipping and insurance to your destination port. Higher quoted price, less coordination on your end.
